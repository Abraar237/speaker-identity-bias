Real speech is a different story. We took recordings of twenty-four real people, from six language backgrounds, reading the same sentences as native speakers. Under a read-aloud rubric, both Gemini judges penalised every single group: Hindi speakers by four to six points, Arabic by about thirteen, Mandarin and Vietnamese by around fifteen. Two judges, in close agreement. The open-weight judge barely reacted at all. Whether that penalty is fair grading of fluency, or something more, is exactly the question this rubric cannot settle. We say so in the paper.
