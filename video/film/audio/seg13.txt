Then we did the experiment every careful reviewer asks for. Every accent you heard so far came from one synthetic voice. So we re-rendered the whole study with six more voices, and asked the same judges again. The penalty dissolved. Across the new voices, the Indian shift is small and indistinguishable from zero. And voice by voice, the story scatters: one voice is up two points, another is down four and a half. The voice, not the accent, moves the score.
